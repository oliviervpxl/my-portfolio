TRNSFORM SHAPER

TRNSFORM SHAPER is a tempo-synchronised VST3 sidechain tool for rhythmic volume ducking and pumping effects. Shape volume with editable curves, sync the movement to your DAW tempo, and reuse your favourite shapes across projects—without setting up a compressor sidechain.

Created by Olivier Vleeschouwers & Bas Engelborghs.

Upcoming Pro Edition

TRNSFORM SHAPER PRO

The upcoming Pro Edition expands the core shaper into a surgical sidechain and multiband dynamics processor.

SYNC | AUDIO | MIDI Sidechain EQ Filter Lookahead Pre-Delay Split-Band Ducking Mid/Side Processing Global Shape Modifiers A/B State Comparison
Feature Descriptions & DSP Details
  1. 3 Trigger Modes [SYNC | AUDIO | MIDI]: Expand beyond standard sync with Triplet, Dotted, and Swing (50–75%) timing. Use sample-accurate MIDI Note-On triggering, or use external audio (Bus 1) with a 0-lag peak envelope detector (18 ms peak-hold).
  2. Sidechain EQ Filter & Real-Time Visualizer: Isolate trigger transients with a 12 dB/oct HPF/LPF filter and Threshold control. View the post-filter signal in real-time (60 FPS) on the Curve Editor background, complete with a Playhead Hit-Flash.
  3. Lookahead Pre-Delay: Selectable 0, 2, 5, or 10 ms lookahead with automatic DAW latency compensation (PDC) for zero-click transient ducking.
  4. Split-Band / Frequency-Specific Ducking: Phase-compensated 24 dB/oct Linkwitz-Riley (LR4) crossover. Duck only the sub/low-bass (20–120 Hz) while leaving mids and highs completely untouched.
  5. Mid/Side Processing: Apply volume shaping to the full stereo signal (L/R), exclusively to the mono center (MID), or exclusively to the stereo edges (SIDE).
  6. Global Shape Modifiers: Warp the entire active curve in real-time with global TENSION and SKEW controls without moving individual nodes.
  7. A/B State Comparison: Instantly compare two complete setups (including all 16 curve slots and all DSP parameters) using [A], [B], and [COPY A>B].
