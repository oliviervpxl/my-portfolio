TRNSFORM SHAPER

TRNSFORM SHAPER is a tempo-synchronised VST3 sidechain tool for rhythmic volume ducking and pumping effects. Shape volume with editable curves, sync the movement to your DAW tempo, and reuse your favourite shapes across projects—without setting up a compressor sidechain.

Created by Olivier Vleeschouwers & Bas Engelborghs.
